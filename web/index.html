ANNO DOMINI · MDXIX · RÍO GUADALQUIVIR
DEPARTURE FROM SEVILLA
The carrack Nuestra Señora de la Victoria warps out into the Guadalquivir at golden hour, a hundred souls aboard — mariners, soldados in steel morions, friars and adventurers — bound for La Española, there to muster the fleet that will carry the conquest to the mainland of the Indies. The first night of a long and bloody enterprise.
